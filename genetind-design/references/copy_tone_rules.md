# GeneTind Copy Tone & Voice Guidelines

This reference defines the verbal identity, microcopy standards, and anti-hype guardrails for GeneTind digital surfaces, interfaces, and marketing outputs.

---

## 1. Core Voice: Scientific, Low-Entropy, Operational

GeneTind speaks like a world-class scientific research instrument: calm, precise, factual, and low-drama.

| Dimension | Approved Approach (Do) | Banned Approach (Don't) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Headline Mood** | *Calm & Factual*: "Run structured checks with clear visual hierarchy." | *Hyped & Desperate*: "Unlock the most powerful workflow ever built!" |
| **Action Labels** | Direct imperative verbs: `Run Check`, `View Details`, `Copy` | Marketing clichés: `Explore Now`, `Grab It`, `Get Started Free` |
| **System Feedback** | Objective status: `Ready`, `Copied`, `Unavailable` | Emotional praise: `Awesome!`, `You're all set!`, `Boom!` |
| **Helper Explanations** | Exactly 1 factual sentence explaining the direct mechanism. | Multi-sentence marketing pitch trying to sell the feature. |

---

## 2. Banned Words & Clichés (Anti-Slop Dictionary)

The following words must **NEVER** appear in GeneTind UI, marketing cards, or documentation:

- ❌ `Unlock`
- ❌ `Magic` / `Magical`
- ❌ `Supercharge`
- ❌ `Unleash`
- ❌ `Revolutionary` / `Disruptive`
- ❌ `Game-changing`
- ❌ `Next-gen` (unless referring to explicit technical biology terminology like Next-Generation Sequencing / NGS)
- ❌ `Effortless` / `Seamless`
- ❌ `Dive in` / `Explore now`

---

## 3. Approved UI Verbs & Button Dictionary

Use concise 1-to-3-word labels:

- Primary Operations: `Run Check`, `Deploy Skill`, `View Details`, `Verify System`, `Open Module`, `Download SVG`, `Export PNG`
- Navigation: `Skip to content`, `Back`, `Documentation`, `Overview`
- Utility & Feedback: `Copy` → `Copied`, `Ready`, `Running`, `Completed`, `Failed`
- Status Meta: `System Label / Ready`, `1:1 Vector Master`, `v1.0`

---

## 4. Writing Rules Summary

1. **Keep it operational**: Microcopy supports the action immediately in front of the user; it does not lecture or self-congratulate.
2. **Keep it short**: Buttons and tags are 1–3 words. Helper text is exactly 1 concise sentence.
3. **Keep it factual**: Error and warning dialogs describe the state, the cause, and the resolution without emotional apology.
